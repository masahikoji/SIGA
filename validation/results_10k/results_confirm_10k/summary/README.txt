All requested scenarios and methods are retained. A partial summary is not a completed validation.
Monte Carlo SEs condition on the shared allocation-covariance estimates.
The primary methods use estimated stratum effects, never DGP effect values.
R_lattice and CRT_lattice use the lattice only for unadjusted binary b=0 superiority.
Elsewhere they use Gaussian R and S tails respectively. Normal-only columns are ablations.
CRT_noise is exploratory and not the primary proposed procedure.
Only null-boundary rows interpret mean S as the variance of the observed statistic.
Reference variances are Monte Carlo estimates from the common inner draws, not exact values.
Scenario-wise intervals are descriptive, not simultaneous multiplicity-adjusted guarantees.
