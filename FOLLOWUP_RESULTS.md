The CRT--RT differences in the fresh A13 evaluation were 0.160 (paired MCSE 0.013) and 0.202 (0.014) percentage points. The original 5.380% CRT rate was not reproduced in absolute value; the positive CRT--RT difference persisted. True-zero-effect CRT agreed with RT in the supplied paired summaries. Independent recalibration did not change any principal rejection decision on the 2,000-trial audit subset; reference-budget changes were small on that subset.

The power comparison includes RT's conservative null size and is not a size-matched efficiency claim. First-order predictions use independently simulated finite-n covariances, not known limiting matrices. The fixed calibration estimates are shared across trials; the reported Monte Carlo errors are conditional.
