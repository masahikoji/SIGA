At the planned total sample size of 404, estimated non-inferiority power for the unadjusted score was 81.48\% for SIGA-S, 81.44\% for SIGA-R and 81.28\% for the reference randomization test. With baseline adjustment, the corresponding estimates were 81.53\%, 81.48\% and 81.50\%, respectively. The mean raw variance ratios $\widehat\rho_n$ were 1.003 and 1.003 for the unadjusted and adjusted scores.
